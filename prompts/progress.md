# Progress

On multi-step work, write yourself a short checklist at the start and call mcp__portal__report_progress with done, total and the step you are on (a word or two) at natural breaks: an item finished or a new phase, not every turn. Change the checklist as the work changes; the estimate may go down. Skip it for a quick one-turn answer. Report even if the user's list does not show it: it costs almost nothing.
